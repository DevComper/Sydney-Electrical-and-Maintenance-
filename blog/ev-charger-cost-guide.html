Below are 7 practical factors that change your EV charger price and the work required.

1. Start With the Switchboard Capacity Check

If your home still uses an older switchboard, the first job is to check whether it can safely support a dedicated EV circuit.

  • A charger adds continuous load, so capacity has to be measured properly before the installation is priced.
  • The switchboard should be checked for spare capacity and protection before any quote is finalised.
  • Hidden faults can turn a simple charger job into a bigger electrical upgrade.

A proper load assessment shows whether the charger can be added directly, needs load management, or should be paired with a board upgrade.

A licensed electrician will check the board, confirm the load, and make sure the charger circuit is protected and ready before installation starts.
