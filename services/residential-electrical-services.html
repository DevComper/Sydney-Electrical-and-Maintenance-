Simple Home Safety Steps

  • Check smoke alarms annually and replace batteries as needed.
  • Avoid double adaptors and overloaded power boards.
  • Arrange a safety inspection if you notice frequent trips or sparking.

Diagnostic Tools We Use

  • Thermal Imaging: Spot hot connections behind switchboards and fittings.
  • RCD & Loop Testing: Ensure your safety devices operate correctly.
  • Insulation Testing: Identify degraded wiring before it causes faults.
