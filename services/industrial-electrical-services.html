Industrial Faults & Maintenance

Heavy-duty equipment needs regular maintenance to avoid downtime.

  • Vibration or Noise from Motors: Can indicate bearing wear or electrical imbalance.
  • Frequent Breaker Trips: May point to overloaded circuits or failing componentry.
  • Control Panel Faults: Faulty starters, relays, or VSD issues that need experienced fault-finding.

We offer planned maintenance programs and on-call fault response for industrial clients.

Industrial Reliability Considerations

Industrial sites need robust systems and scheduled maintenance to avoid costly downtime and safety incidents.

  • Motor & Control Maintenance: Regular inspections of motor starters, VSDs and contactors prevent unplanned outages.
  • Panel Servicing: Scheduled cleaning and thermal checks reduce risk of failure under heavy loads.
  • Spare Parts Strategy: Stocking common spares for critical equipment reduces MTTR (mean time to repair).
  • Safety Systems: Emergency stops, interlocks and proper earthing are essential for compliance.
  • Planned Shutdowns: We coordinate maintenance windows to limit production impact.

How to Reduce Production Interruptions

  • Implement regular maintenance schedules for critical plant.
  • Use thermal imaging to detect hotspots in control panels.
  • Keep key spares on-site to speed repairs.
  • Test safety interlocks and emergency systems regularly.
  • Plan shutdowns with clear timelines and communication.
