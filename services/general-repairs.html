Common Causes We Diagnose

Many repair jobs are caused by ageing components, moisture, or simple installation faults â€” finding the root cause saves repeat visits.

  • Worn or Damaged Wiring: Insulation breakdown and rodent damage can create shorts and nuisance tripping.
  • Faulty Switches & Outlets: Loose contacts and worn parts cause intermittent faults and safety risks.
  • Corroded Connections: Moisture in outdoor or poorly sealed fittings accelerates failure.
  • Overloaded Circuits: New appliances or poor load distribution lead to frequent trips.
  • Missing Safety Devices: Absence of RCDs or outdated protection increases risk of shock and appliance damage.

How to Prevent Common Electrical Problems

  • Avoid overloading multi-socket adapters and run high-load appliances on separate circuits.
  • Replace cracked or discoloured outlets â€” they are a sign of heat build-up.
  • Book a safety inspection every 2 years or after major renovations.
  • Keep outdoor fittings sealed and clear of debris to prevent moisture ingress.
  • Use qualified electricians for any wiring or modification work.

How We Diagnose Problems

  • Thermal Imaging: Identify hot spots behind switches or in wiring runs.
  • Insulation & Continuity Tests: Find damaged cables and poor connections.
  • Voltage & Current Measurements: Detect overloaded circuits and intermittent faults.
  • Visual and Mechanical Checks: Catch loose terminals and damaged fittings quickly.
  • Clear Reporting: We explain faults and options in plain English with fixed quotes.
