How Our Power Restoration Service Works

We follow a safety-first approach to restore power and make lasting repairs.

  • Immediate Triage: We assess affected circuits and restore power to critical areas where safe.
  • Fault Finding: Pinpoint the root cause using test gear and inspection.
  • Repair & Test: Replace faulty components, repair wiring, and test the system before sign-off.
