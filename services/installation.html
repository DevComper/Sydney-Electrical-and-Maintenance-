Common Installation Pitfalls

Good installations avoid common mistakes that lead to repeated faults or safety issues. We design work to last.

  • Undersized Conductors: Using small cable for high-load circuits causes overheating and nuisance tripping.
  • Poor Terminations: Loose or double-lugged terminals create heat and failure points.
  • Insufficient Support: Inadequate mounting and cable support leads to mechanical failure over time.
  • Non-compliant Components: Using unapproved fittings or cheap parts increases safety risks.
  • Missing Documentation: Lack of test certificates complicates future work and compliance.

How to Ensure Long-Lasting Installations

  • Follow manufacturer ratings for cables, fittings and fixtures.
  • Use correct protective devices sized to the circuit.
  • Label and document circuits and certificates on completion.
  • Schedule regular inspections after major works.
  • Engage licensed electricians for any permanent wiring changes.

Tools & Compliance Checks

  • Insulation Resistance Tests: Verify cable integrity before handover.
  • Earth Continuity Testing: Ensure safety earthing and bonding is correct.
  • Thermal Scans: Spot poor terminations before they fail.
  • Certificate Issuing: We provide compliance paperwork and test results on completion.
