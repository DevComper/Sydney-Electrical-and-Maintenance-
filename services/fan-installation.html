Signs You Need Fan Repair or Replacement

Not all fan issues require a full replacement. Spotting the problem early keeps your home comfortable and prevents further damage.

  • Excessive Wobble: A fan that wobbles indicates an imbalanced blade or loose mounting and should be fixed before it causes damage.
  • Unusual Noises: Squeaks, grinding or humming often point to worn bearings or loose components.
  • Fan Won't Start or Stalls: Electrical faults or failing capacitors can prevent the motor from running reliably.
  • Lights Flicker or Switch Issues: Problems with integrated light kits or switches are often simple to diagnose and repair.

If you notice any of these signs, book a diagnosis â€” our electricians fix most fan problems on the first visit.

Common Fan Problems & Causes

Understanding typical fan issues helps us diagnose quickly and fix them correctly.

  • Loose Mounting: Incorrect or loose mounting hardware leads to wobble and noise.
  • Worn Bearings or Motor: Old motors or bearings make noise and reduce airflow.
  • Electrical Faults: Faulty capacitors, switches or wiring can stop the fan or cause intermittent operation.
  • Poor Balancing: Bent blades or dust buildup cause imbalance and vibration.
  • Improper Installation: Incorrect wiring or inadequate support creates safety hazards.

How to Prevent Fan Problems & Extend Life

  • Have fans mounted to a fan-rated box or solid joist anchor.
  • Keep blades clean and free of heavy dust buildup.
  • Balance blades after installation to avoid wobble and noise.
  • Service bearings and replace worn capacitors when performance drops.
  • Use surge-protected circuits where motors run frequently.
