Signs You Need Lighting Repair

  • Lights Flicker or Dim: Flickering often indicates driver or connection faults.
  • Burnt Smell or Discolouration: Any smell of burning or visible scorching needs immediate attention.
  • Downlights Fail to Turn On: Likely driver or wiring issues requiring diagnosis.

We troubleshoot and repair lighting faults with safe, permanent solutions.

What Causes Lighting Faults?

Lighting faults usually arise from driver failure, wiring issues, or poor terminations â€” all of which we diagnose and repair.

  • Failed LED Drivers: LED modules rely on drivers that can fail before the fixture does.
  • Poor Connections: Loose terminals and poor crimping cause flicker and overheating.
  • Incorrect Dimmer Compatibility: Old dimmers may not work with modern LED drivers.
  • Water Ingress: Outdoor fittings exposed to moisture corrode and fail prematurely.
  • Bulb and Lamp Failures: Replaceable lamps and faulty fittings are frequently the cause of outages.

How to Prevent Lighting Faults

  • Use compatible dimmers specified for LED drivers.
  • Install outdoor fixtures with proper IP ratings and sealing.
  • Replace damaged drivers and retrofit with quality components.
  • Schedule routine checks on high-use or hard-to-reach fittings.
  • Never attempt LED driver replacement without a qualified electrician.

Lighting Diagnostics & Tools

  • Driver Testing: On-site checks to quickly identify failed LED drivers.
