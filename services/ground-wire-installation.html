When You Need Grounding Work

Grounding issues often surface as equipment damage, stray voltages or nuisance tripping.

  • Frequent RCD Tripping: Poor earthing can contribute to nuisance trips or unsafe leakage currents.
  • Appliance Damage: Sensitive electronics may fail if the earthing system is inadequate.
  • Corroded Earth Connections: Outdoor connections exposed to moisture can degrade over time.

We test and repair existing earthing systems or install new compliant earth arrangements where required.

Our Grounding & Testing Process

  • Inspect existing earth conductors and clamp connections.
  • Measure earth loop and soil resistivity where required.
  • Install or replace earth rods, conductors and bonding as necessary.
  • Provide test certification and documentation.

Grounding Maintenance Tips

  • Inspect earth clamps and bonding for corrosion annually.
  • Keep earth rods clear of surface runoff and vegetation.
  • Retest earth loop and soil resistivity after major works.

Testing Equipment & Certification

  • Earth Resistance Testers: Measure and confirm effective grounding.
  • Soil Resistivity Kits: Used where required to design compliant earthing systems.
  • Clamp & Continuity Testers: Ensure bonding and equipotential connections are secure.
  • Documentation: Provide test certificates suitable for compliance and insurance.
