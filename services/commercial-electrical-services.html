How to Avoid Downtime

  • Schedule regular preventative maintenance to spot issues early.
  • Perform load assessments before adding heavy equipment.
  • Install surge protection to safeguard sensitive electronics.
  • Keep emergency lighting and RCDs tested and up to date.
  • Have a priority emergency response plan for business-critical systems.
